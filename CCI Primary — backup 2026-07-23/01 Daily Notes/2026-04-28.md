- **Philly Codefest**

College Assembly - **Slide 7**


On April 11th and 12th CCI hosted our 13th annual Philly Codefest, a software hackathon.

This year’s theme was _Building AI for Philly’s Future_, with teams developing AI-powered solutions focused on real challenges impacting Philadelphia communities.

We had 375 participants and sponsors representing Drexel and 16 other colleges and universities.

COEC students made up 80% of all participants, which was great to see and really shows strong engagement across CCI, Engineering, and Biomed.

Despite increasing the minimum team size, we had a record 78 projects presented during Sunday’s science fair.

We awarded $12,250 in prize money, along with additional experiential opportunities provided by our sponsors.

CSL, Comcast, Penn Mutual, Apollo, and Wexford returned as sponsors this year, and we welcomed AppliedAI as a new sponsor.

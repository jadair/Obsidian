# CCI Apps Vault

This vault is organized around a simple workflow:

- `00 Inbox` for fast capture
- `01 Daily Notes` for day-level planning and scratch work
- `02 Projects` for active initiatives with clear outcomes
- `03 Areas` for ongoing responsibilities
- `04 Resources` for reusable reference material
- `99 Archive` for inactive or completed material[[2026-04-06]]
- `999 Templates` for repeatable note structures

## Operating Rules

- Capture quickly in `00 Inbox`, then move or rewrite notes into the right folder.
- Keep project work in project folders instead of scattered daily notes.
- Keep durable reference material in `04 Resources`, not inside project notes.
- Archive finished projects instead of deleting them.
- Rename vague notes quickly. Avoid leaving scratch notes at the vault root.

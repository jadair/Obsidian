# Daily Workflow

The day-to-day rhythm for operating this vault. See [[00 Home/Working Methodology|Working Methodology]] for the principles behind it, and [[00 Home/Decisions|Decisions]] for how to make and record decisions specifically.

## Start of Day

- Open (or create) today's note in `02 Daily Notes` using the daily template.
- Fill in `Priorities` with the one or two things that most need to happen today — not a copy of the full task backlog. If everything is a priority, nothing is.
- Skim [[00 Home/Active Projects|Active Projects]] for anything time-sensitive.
- Check `01 Inbox` — if it's short, clear it before diving into work.

## During the Day

- Capture fast, in the moment, wherever you are: today's daily note for anything tied to today, or `01 Inbox/Capture.md` for anything that doesn't have an obvious home yet. Don't stop to file things properly mid-task.
- Protect time for the priority item before it gets consumed by meetings and small requests — everything conspires against protected time, so guard at least one uninterrupted block for it.
- When a meeting happens, start it from the [[07 Templates/meeting-template|meeting template]] (or [[07 Templates/one-on-one-template|one-on-one template]] for 1:1s) directly in `03 Meetings/<year>/`, even if you fill it in lightly during the meeting and flesh it out after. Every meeting should produce at least one of: a decision, an action item with an owner, or an explicit "no decision needed" — a meeting that produces none of the three probably didn't need to happen.

## End of Day / End of Week

- Process `01 Inbox` to zero: move each note to its real home (see [[00 Home/Working Methodology#Where Does This Go? Filing Rules|the filing rules]]), or delete it if it turned out not to matter.
- Skim the week's daily notes for anything that should have been promoted into a project, area, resource, or meeting record but wasn't yet.
- Update project dashboards (`Home.md`/status sections and each project's **Roadmap**/**Deliverables** tables) for anything that moved forward.
- Update [[00 Home/Roadmap|Roadmap]]'s cross-project snapshot from each project dashboard, and pull forward anything newly due soon into its Deliverables Coming Due table. This is the one deliberate sync step between Obsidian and JIRA/GitHub — see [[00 Home/Tool Boundaries|Tool Boundaries]].
- **Run a weekly review.** Look back at where your time actually went (per the daily notes) versus what you'd planned to prioritize. Ask again: is the work in `04 Projects` and `05 Areas` still the highest-leverage use of time, or has something drifted into busywork? Adjust `Priorities` for the coming week accordingly.

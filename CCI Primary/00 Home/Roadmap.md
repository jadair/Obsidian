# Roadmap

The one place to look for a holistic view of workload, deliverables, and where each project actually stands — so you don't have to open every project folder or switch into JIRA/GitHub to get oriented.

**How to use this:** JIRA is the live source of truth for status once the [[00 Home/Tool Boundaries|GitHub-JIRA integration]] is set up — open your JIRA board for real-time status. This note is the lightweight, always-in-Obsidian version: update it during your weekly review (see [[00 Home/Daily Workflow|Daily Workflow]]) so you have a fast-loading snapshot without needing to context-switch tools just to remember what's in flight.

# CCI Apps Vault

This vault is organized around a lightweight PARA workflow:

- `01 Inbox` for fast capture and unprocessed notes
- `02 Daily Notes` for day-level planning and scratch work
- `03 Meetings` for meeting records, organized by year
- `04 Projects` for active initiatives with a defined outcome
- `05 Areas` for ongoing responsibilities without a completion date
- `06 Resources` for reusable reference material
- `07 Templates` for repeatable note structures
- `08 Archive` for inactive or completed material

## Operating Rules

- Capture quickly in `01 Inbox`, then move or rewrite notes into the right folder.
- Keep project work in project folders instead of scattered daily notes.
- Keep durable reference material in `06 Resources`, not inside project notes.
- Move formal meeting records to `03 Meetings`, linked to their project or area.
- Archive finished projects instead of deleting them.
- Rename vague notes quickly. Avoid leaving scratch notes at the vault root.

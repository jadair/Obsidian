Hello everyone,

Welcome to Week 2. I hope everyone had a great weekend!

This week we will be focusing on data warehouses, dimensional models, business intelligence, and big data. As you work through the lecture video and the star schema example video, pay close attention to the difference between operational data and decision-support data, as well as how fact tables, dimension tables, measures, keys, and grain all fit together in a dimensional model.

## TONE & MESSAGING GUIDELINES

**Do:**

- Be aspirational yet grounded in real impact
- Focus on outcomes and career opportunities
- Emphasize hands-on, practical learning
- Highlight interdisciplinary collaboration
- Celebrate faculty expertise and research
- Acknowledge the regional innovation ecosystem (Cellicon Valley)

**Don't:**

- Oversell hype; stay factual about the field
- Get too technical in web copy (save details for official Drexel pages)
- Duplicate official Drexel academic content; link instead
- Use jargon without explanation for general audiences

**Key Messages to Weave Throughout:**

- "Next generation of therapeutics"
- "Hands-on, practical education"
- "Real-world impact"
- "Flexible pathways for all students"
- "At the heart of Cellicon Valley"
- "Interdisciplinary expertise"
- "From classroom to clinic"
